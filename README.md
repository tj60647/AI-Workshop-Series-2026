# AI Workshop Series 2026

A series of three hands-on workshops that build a practical foundation for designers working with AI. We move from observing behavior to assembling systems to authoring and evaluating what those systems do. Each workshop is three hours — part lecture and demonstration, part doing, discussing, and comparing notes. They work as a sequence, but each stands on its own.

---

## Workshops

### [Workshop 1: AI Literacy — How It Actually Works](./workshop-01-ai-literacy/README.md)

These systems predict language. In this hands-on workshop, we'll look at what that means in practice: how models sample and generate text, how settings shape the output, and why the same prompt behaves differently across different models. You'll work in a sandbox, testing models side by side, writing instructions, and exploring how knowledge bases let you ground AI in your own content.

**No technical background required.**

---

### [Workshop 2: AI Prototyping Pipeline — From Prompt to Production](./workshop-02-ai-prototyping-pipeline/README.md)

This is the plate tectonics workshop — the ground is moving and it moves fast. We'll walk through a full prototyping pipeline — from AI Studio to GitHub to Vercel — and you'll ship a working project in a single session. Along the way, we'll cover how to connect your own content through Google Drive, how to write better prompts using structured vocabulary and glossaries, and how vibecoding actually works in practice.

**Some comfort with web tools is helpful but not required.**

---

### [Workshop 3: AI as Design Material — Where the Design Decisions Are](./workshop-03-ai-as-design-material/README.md)

AI systems are full of design decisions that shape human experience — what gets said, how it responds, what it remembers, what it ignores. This workshop focuses on where those decisions live and how designers can work with them. The observation and building from the first two workshops become the basis for critique.
