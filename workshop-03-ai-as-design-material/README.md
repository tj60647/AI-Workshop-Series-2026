# Workshop 3: AI as Design Material — Where the Design Decisions Are

**Duration:** 3 hours
**Format:** Part lecture and case discussion, part collaborative critique

---

## Overview

AI systems are full of design decisions that shape human experience — what gets said, how it responds, what it remembers, what it ignores. The design surfaces of AI — instructions, parameters, knowledge, tools — now become the things you assemble and test.

This workshop focuses on where those decisions live and how designers can work with them. The observation and building from the first two workshops become the basis for critique: we look at the same design surfaces — now as authored structures whose behavior can be evaluated, discussed, and improved.

We'll examine where the meaningful choices live, how to recognize and name them, and how to share and critique this work the way we already do with other design materials.

The first half is lecture and case discussion; the second half is collaborative — mapping decisions, comparing perspectives, and practicing the shared language that makes this work discussable.

---

## Learning Goals

- Recognize AI systems as designed artifacts with explicit and implicit decisions
- Map the four design surfaces — **instructions**, **parameters**, **knowledge**, **tools** — as authored structures
- Practice naming and evaluating design decisions in AI outputs
- Apply a shared critical vocabulary to AI work
- Connect AI critique to existing design critique practices

## The Four Design Surfaces

| Surface | What it is | Where authorship lives |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Instructions** | System prompts and behavioral directives | Tone, task scope, persona, constraints |
| **Parameters** | Temperature, sampling settings, model choice | Risk, creativity, consistency |
| **Knowledge** | Retrieval sources, grounding documents | Accuracy, domain, perspective |
| **Tools** | Functions, APIs, external capabilities | Actions the system can take |
